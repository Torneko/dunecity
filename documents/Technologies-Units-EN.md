# Unit technologies — Dune Legacy Tornie 1.0.533

Vanilla, Tornie, Tornie Lite and Jericho. These tables describe a base developed from its own Construction Yard, without captured factories or advanced buildings supplied by the scenario.

Game technology level (1–9) and factory upgrade count (0, 1, 2…) are separate requirements. Credits, terrain and unit limits still apply. Higher levels retain previous unlocks.

Vanilla Kleshmersh uses Neutral’s technology tree. Tornie and Jericho offer twelve named factions; Tornie Lite offers six.

Faction codes: H = Harkonnen, A = Atreides, O = Ordos, F = Fremen, S = Sardaukar, M = Mercenary, N = Neutral, R = Rebels, C = Corruptique, W = Wildspade, K = Kleshmersh, T = Tharpique.

## Special production rules

A newly placed Refinery normally supplies an initial Harvester before regular factory production becomes available.

The Worfinery produces Troopers, Trooper Squads and Harvesters as soon as the building is available, with no extra unit-specific requirements.

Wildspade normally unlocks IX at level 6 through the Starport chain, even though IX’s configured technology level is 1.

The Chemical Carryall requires IX and two High-Tech Factory upgrades. Atreides cannot normally obtain the second upgrade in Tornie or Tornie Lite. Wildspade unlocks it at level 6 in Tornie and level 7 in Jericho.

The Starport uses CHOAM stock rather than normal factory unlocks. It excludes the Chemical Carryall and excludes the Ornithopter during campaigns. Delivery offers are not guaranteed.

Palace abilities become available at level 8. Ordos and Mercenary can obtain Saboteurs. Love Factory and Chaos Factory provide additional delivery offers at level 9 in the Tornie mods.

Captured factories may provide their original faction’s technology. INI duplicate keys follow the engine’s first-occurrence rule.
