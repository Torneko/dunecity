## Jericho Lite

Jericho Lite: six Tornie Lite factions and technology, all four Jericho spice families enabled by default with the same colors and effects, including in the editor and generator. Its six opening missions copy Tornie Lite’s terrain, economy, structures and forces; only the opponents follow Jericho Lite’s distinct plan.

## Common co-op campaign

From the main menu, “Co-op campaign” opens a LAN or Internet lobby for two players. Each player chooses a faction and controls a separate base and army on the same team. Both players may choose the same faction.

Co-op follows the nine stages of the original campaign for the faction chosen by the host at launch. Its terrain, structures, opposing forces and objectives are retained. The source campaign stays fixed when resuming; progress and checkpoints remain independent of solo.

The main player keeps the original base and starting units. The partner receives matching forces and credits, plus an extra MCV on free rock near the main Construction Yard, to deploy a separate base. Special spawns and unavailable units use an equivalent compatible with the partner’s faction.

Harvest objectives are shared: spice stored by both players is added together, with a common target of twice the solo quota. Both computers calculate the same team victory.

Available factions: 12 in Tornie and Jericho, 6 in Tornie Lite and Jericho Lite, 9 in Vanilla. The 45 source campaigns provide 405 co-op variants, with nine stages each.

Commander awards, campaign conquest and Chaos Conqueror concern solo campaigns. Finishing the common campaign does not complete the factions’ solo campaigns.

Version 1.0.538 adds a guest WOR in new intros, on free rock near the host WOR. The host can choose enemy AI types and difficulties, replace the guest with an AI and enable Allied control (off by default). Enemy faction conflicts are resolved and colors remain distinct.

In the host lobby, Load a save offers Manual and Automatic files. The lobby reopens on the saved mission and the guest rejoins. Tornie confirmed this loading works. Older checkpoints retain their positions; the new WOR layouts require a new campaign or subsequent mission.

Both PCs need version 1.0.538 and the same mod, with network protocol 6. Automated Windows/Linux tests pass; extended playtesting remains useful. [Full co-op guide](Cooperative-Campaign-1.0.538-EN.md).
